Gabbai app · phase 2, seat map

The hall is redrawn from the Excel chart, layout only: 5 blocks, rows 0–13, the bimah in the middle, about 205 seats. The names in the chart (Shabbat seats) are not used. The whole hall is too wide for 48dp seats on a phone, so the map has two levels: a mini-map of the whole hall, and the chosen block in large tiles. Example names; days and seats are invented.
