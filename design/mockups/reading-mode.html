Gabbai app · reading mode (draft)

One page for the call-ups only: from "ויעזור… כהן קרב, יעמוד" to "יעמדו המגביה והגולל". It has the gabbai's words in full (warm boxes) and the Torah text of each aliyah. Nothing for the ציבור or the ש״ץ. The oleh's blessings (printed at the bimah), Mi Sheberach and the other rare texts are chips in "תוספות" at the end, each opening its own screen, so the page reads call, reading, call, reading. Real text: Monday of וירא, 26.10.2026 (Miqra according to the Masorah, CC-BY-SA). Names are invented.
