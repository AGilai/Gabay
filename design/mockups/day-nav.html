מעבר בין ימים, ומניין נוסף

Design for review, before any code. A day row under the title on the main page: the arrows go to the previous or next day with a reading (Mon, Thu, Rosh Chodesh, fasts…), showing the date they lead to. When the last oleh is marked, a "הקריאה הסתיימה" card offers the next reading or another minyan today. A past day opens filled in and locked. Today and future days can have a second minyan, with a switch between them. Example: today is Wednesday, Rosh Chodesh Kislev (4 olim). Names are invented.

מונחים

בממשקמה זה
→ ל׳ חשווןPrevious day with a reading. In Hebrew, earlier is to the right.
ב׳ כסלו ←Next day with a reading. Days without a reading are skipped.
היוםShown in the middle of the row on any other day. One tap goes back to today.
הקריאה הסתיימהThe card after the last "עלה ✓".
+ מניין נוסףOpens a second list for the same day. Today and future days only.
מניין 1 · מניין 2Switch between the lists of the same day. "✓" shows the finished one.
קריאה שעברה · תקןA past day is locked. "תקן" unlocks it to fix a mistake.

שאלות אליך

  1. Arrows skip days with no reading?Recommended: yes. From Thursday, "הבא" goes straight to Monday. Sunday, Tuesday and so on are reached only through the calendar (היסטוריה).
  2. Past day: locked, with "תקן" to edit?Recommended: yes, so a stray tap doesn't change history. Today "היסטוריה" opens past days unlocked.
  3. Second minyan and the turns:An oleh in minyan 2 counts like any oleh (his days reset). Someone who already went up in minyan 1 today is marked "✓ עלה במניין 1" on the choosing page, as we do now for people in the reading.
  4. The word:"מניין 1 / מניין 2"? Or "מניין א׳ / ב׳", or by hour ("6:15 / 7:00")?
