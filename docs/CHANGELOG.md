# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-23), alpha

First version for the gabbai's own phone.

- Main page: today's reading (Israel calendar), 3 | 4 switch, אין כהן / אין לוי, one-tap suggestion, "עלה ✓".
- Choosing page: Kohanim / Leviim / Israel by longest wait; obligations first; occasional people not seen for
  6 months collapsed; search by name, calling line or hint; long press opens the person card.
- Person card and quick add; "הפסק להציע"; obligations for today; yahrzeits.
- Prayer texts with names filled in (placeholder wording until the shul's nusach Sfard is entered in settings).
- Sick list, history (fix or add past readings), backup and restore, one-time WhatsApp import.
- Installable and offline (PWA); WhatsApp "share" sends an export straight to the import screen.
