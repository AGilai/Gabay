# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Seat areas: 5 columns × קדימה / אחורה ("3-ק"). Set on the person card or in עוד → סידור מקומות; shown on the
  choosing list; a "מקום" filter in search.
- Mi Sheberach for the sick, asked for out loud (no name chosen): a גבר · אישה · כמה חולים switch.
- Sick list: add with name · בן / בת · mother's name; the word בן / בת picks the man's or the woman's blessing.
- Yoledet (son): "וְאֶת בְּנָהּ שֶׁנּוֹלַד לָהּ".
- Search is its own screen with filters: כהנים / לויים / ישראל (the tab you came from is already picked),
  and שם העולה / שם האב (split at "בן").
- The choosing page keeps the days number for people already in the reading and marks them instead.
- Reading mode (מצב קריאה): the whole call-up as one scroll, from "ויעזור… כהן קרב, יעמוד" to "יעמדו המגביה
  והגולל", with each aliyah's Torah text after its call. Adds הכהן / הלוי when a line lacks it, and
  שני / שלישי / רביעי / במקום כהן. "החלף" swaps an oleh and comes back. Additions as chips at the end.
- Main page: "▶ התחל קריאה"; the 3 | 4 switch is gone (the calendar decides).
- Sick: one blessing per person (man's or woman's wording) by default, or all together.
- New texts: ברכות התורה and ברכת הגומל (Siddur Sfard, Metsudah, CC-BY).
- Setting: Mi Sheberach for the oleh after each aliyah in reading mode (off by default).

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-23), alpha

First version for the gabbai's own phone.

- Main page: today's reading (Israel calendar), 3 | 4 switch, אין כהן / אין לוי, one-tap suggestion, "עלה ✓".
- Choosing page: Kohanim / Leviim / Israel by longest wait; obligations first; occasional people not seen for
  6 months collapsed; search by name, calling line or hint; long press opens the person card.
- Person card and quick add; "הפסק להציע"; obligations for today; yahrzeits.
- Prayer texts with names filled in (placeholder wording until the shul's nusach Sfard is entered in settings).
- Sick list, history (fix or add past readings), backup and restore, one-time WhatsApp import.
- Installable and offline (PWA); WhatsApp "share" sends an export straight to the import screen.
